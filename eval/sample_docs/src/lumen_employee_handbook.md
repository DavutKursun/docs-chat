# Lumen Analytics — Employee Handbook

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Welcome

This handbook explains the main policies that apply to every employee of Lumen Analytics ("the Company"). It is not a contract. Where this handbook and your employment agreement differ, the employment agreement applies. Questions about any policy should go to People Operations (people@lumen-analytics.example).

## 2. Employment basics

### 2.1 Probation period

New employees have a probation period of three months. During probation either side may end the employment with one week of notice. At the end of probation your manager holds a review meeting and confirms the position in writing.

### 2.2 Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, normally Monday to Friday between 09:00 and 18:00 with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours, when everyone is expected to be available, are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours you may arrange your start and end times with your manager.

### 2.3 Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesday is the company-wide office day and everyone is expected on site. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the Head of People and are reviewed every six months. Remote employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of 400 EUR, paid with the first salary after the arrangement is approved.

## 3. Time off

### 3.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month. New employees who start during the year receive leave in proportion to the months remaining. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March; any days beyond that are lost.

### 3.2 Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the country where the employee is based. Employees who are asked to work on a public holiday receive one compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

### 3.3 Sick leave

If you are unwell, inform your manager before 10:00 on the first day. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence. The Company pays full salary for up to 30 sick days per year.

### 3.4 Parental leave

Birth and adoptive parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Requests should be submitted at least 8 weeks in advance where possible.

## 4. Pay and expenses

### 4.1 Salary

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month by bank transfer. Payslips are available in the HR portal. Salary reviews take place once a year, in February, with any change effective from 1 April.

### 4.2 Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when submitted through the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase, with a receipt attached. Single expenses above 250 EUR need prior written approval from your manager. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day. Taxis are reimbursed only when public transport is not reasonably available or when travelling after 22:00.

### 4.3 Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop and a monitor. Equipment remains Company property and must be returned on the last working day. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## 5. Conduct

### 5.1 Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated and may lead to dismissal. Concerns can be raised with your manager, with People Operations, or anonymously through the reporting form linked on the intranet.

### 5.2 Confidentiality and data

Customer data may only be accessed for a documented business purpose. Never store customer data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Report any suspected data breach to security@lumen-analytics.example immediately; the security team must be able to notify affected customers within 72 hours.

### 5.3 Conflicts of interest

Outside employment or consulting that overlaps with the Company's business requires written approval from the Head of People. Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than 50 EUR must be declined or reported.

## 6. Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences, plus up to 5 paid learning days. Unused budget does not carry over. Conference attendance abroad must be approved by your manager at least 4 weeks in advance.

## 7. Leaving the Company

The notice period after probation is one month for employees and two months for managers. Accrued but unused annual leave is paid out with the final salary. An exit interview with People Operations is offered to everyone who leaves.

## 8. Changes to this handbook

The Company may update this handbook at any time. Material changes are announced by email at least two weeks before they take effect. The current version is always available on the intranet.
